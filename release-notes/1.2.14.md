# Hormachuelos Optimized 1.2.14

This release turns longer AI work into a controlled, recoverable mission rather than a single opaque chat turn.

## Three flagship features

- **Mission Control** persists a project objective, audience, non-negotiables, definition of done, execution depth, approval policy, and allowed actions. Adaptive Director carries that contract through every phase without overwriting the user's saved mode.
- **Workspace Time Machine** records bounded, privacy-safe action summaries for mutating runs. Users can inspect what changed, undo the last action, or roll back a run without silently overwriting newer work.
- **Test & Fix Everything** discovers the project's real checks and follows a closed inspect, test, repair, and retest loop. Results must be reported as Passed, Fixed then passed, Blocked, or Not applicable; unverified work cannot be presented as proof.

## Also improved

- Mission Control is available directly from the main header and the live Director strip.
- The Director now exposes current phase, progress, status, Stop, Time Machine, and Mission controls.
- Ask, Research, Plan, Build, Multi-Agent, and Adaptive routing keep their specialized behavior while supporting trusted one-run overrides.
- AI completion cards and the Director strip remain readable when the native Preview narrows the workspace.
- Opening Mission Control now closes the native Preview first, preventing the Preview WebView from obscuring the mission dossier.
- Gemini CLI model discovery and authenticated Google-account support are included without exposing OAuth tokens.
