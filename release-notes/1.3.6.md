# Hormachuelos Optimized 1.3.6

## Persistent turn transcript

Every step of a run now stays on the record in Ask mode.

- Status and reasoning steps persist as a compact step log under your message instead of being replaced as work progresses.
- Status steps appear as `› …` rows; streamed reasoning is collected into `● …` thought rows.
- Duplicate consecutive status updates collapse into one row so the log stays readable.
- The log resets with each new message and is not shown for restored sessions.
- The Build timeline and the AGENTIC Execution Workbench keep their existing layouts.

Steps stay on the record.
